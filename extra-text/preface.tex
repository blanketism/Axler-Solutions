This solutions manual accompanies the fourth edition of Sheldon Axler's \emph{Linear Algebra Done Right}.
While several community-maintained solution sets exist for the 3\textsuperscript{rd} edition, the 4\textsuperscript{th} edition introduces enough new and reorganized material to warrant a dedicated solutions manual.

This manual is written primarily for students encountering rigorous linear algebra for the first time.
Axler's text is aimed at students who are new to the subject but have developed mathematical maturity and proof-writing skills through prior coursework.
Additionally, the text places particular emphasis on analysis-inspired ideas and reasoning, which may be unfamiliar to students whose prior exposure to linear algebra has been primarily computational.
Thus, I have attempted to write with an emphasis on clarity, accessibility, and completeness.

This manual is meant to be a companion to, rather than a replacement for, reading the text and attempting the exercises independently.
Linear algebra--particularly in Axler's determinant-free and operator-theoretic approach--is best learned through active engagement with the material.
Hence, I strongly encourage readers to attempt each exercise on their own before consulting the solutions provided here.

Each exercise is presented verbatim as it appears in the text, along with any author-provided hints or notes.
Exercises marked with \textcolor{red!70}{$\bigstar$} contain alternate solutions relegated to the appendix, which may provide additional insight or a different perspective on the problem.

Despite my best efforts to ensure the accuracy of the solutions presented in this manual, I cannot guarantee that they are free from errors.
Corrections, suggestions, and feedback are always welcome.
If you notice a mistake or have a question, I encourage you to reach out through the channels listed above.

Finally, I would like to express my gratitude to Sheldon Axler for writing \emph{Linear Algebra Done Right}, a book that has had a profound influence on my understanding and appreciation of linear algebra.
This manual is a labor of love, and I hope that it serves as a useful companion for students working through the text and developing a deeper understanding of linear algebra.
